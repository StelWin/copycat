Questions

Before you sign up.

What does early access cost?

Nothing. It's being built right now, and the people on this list get in first and free while that's true. If it ever becomes paid, you'll be told before anything changes.

Do I need to be a designer?

No. You need a reference you like and a client whose brand you know. Copycat does the rebuilding; you decide what's right.

Isn't this just copying someone's work?

You're borrowing a layout, the way every designer always has — not reposting someone's photo or their words. What comes back is your client's product, your client's colours and your client's copy in a structure you liked.

When does it open?

No date to promise yet, which is exactly why there's a list instead of a countdown. You'll get one email when it's ready to use.

What happens to my email?

It's used to tell you when Copycat opens, and for nothing else. No list is sold, shared or rented. The privacy page says it plainly.
