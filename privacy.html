Privacy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Copycat is not open yet. The only thing this website does is collect an email address from people who want to know when it is, plus two optional answers. This page says exactly what that means. It is short because there is very little to describe.

Who is responsible

This site is run by the person building Copycat. For anything on this page, write to CONTACT_EMAIL and you will get a reply.

What is collected

  • Your email address, when you type it into the form and submit it.
  • Two optional answers — what you make posts for, and roughly how many a month — only if you choose to tap them after signing up.

That is the complete list. There is no account, no password, no payment information, no uploaded files, and nothing is asked for that is not on this page.

Why

The email address is used for one purpose: to tell you when Copycat is ready to use, and occasionally to share progress while it is being built. The two optional answers are used to decide what gets built first. Nothing is used for anything else.

The legal basis is your consent — you typed it in and pressed a button — and you can take that consent back at any time.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are handled by Formspree, which stores them so they can be passed on. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs including visitors' IP addresses.

Your address is never sold, rented, traded or handed to anyone for their own marketing. There is no advertising network involved in this site.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party trackers. That is why there is no cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to.

How long it is kept

Until Copycat opens and you have been told, or until you ask for it to be deleted, whichever comes first. If the project is abandoned, the list is deleted and you will be told that it has been.

Your rights

You can ask to see what is held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it erased. Ask at CONTACT_EMAIL and it will be done, without being made difficult. Every email sent also carries a one-click unsubscribe link, which removes you immediately.

If you are in the UK or the EU and you think this has been handled badly, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it, and anyone already on the list is told by email before the change takes effect.
